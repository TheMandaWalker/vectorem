Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to connect.

Where is my data?

On your PC, in your user folder (%APPDATA%\Vectorem). It doesn't go to a server. The app checks at most once a day for a new version; you can turn that check off in Settings.

Do I need the Android app?

No, Vectorem for Windows works on its own. With a paired phone you get sync, file sending and the remote.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Because the preview installer isn't signed with a certificate yet. That's planned before the public release.

What about Mac or Linux?

Not for now. Windows first.

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings › Apps › Vectorem. Your data stays in %APPDATA%\Vectorem until you delete that folder.
