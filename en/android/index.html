The idea

Turn on what you use

Every tool is a module. Your dashboard only shows yours.

Three screens to start

At first launch, you say what you often do. Vectorem turns on the right tools and sets up your home.

A home of your own

Today's tasks, streaks, pinned notes, next event, weekly stats. Hide and reorder as you like.

Guided, not pushy

A bubble on your first visit to each screen, and a “?” in every tool to see how it works again.

On your home screen

Streaks, Today, Live tool and Quick launch widgets. Tick things off without opening the app.

Privacy

Your data stays with you

No account

Nothing to sign up for: no e-mail, no password.

On your phone

Notes, tasks, streaks and recordings are stored on the device. You can export them to a backup file.

Permission per tool

Microphone, camera or notifications are asked by the tool that needs them. Saying no only blocks that tool.

No forced ads

Optional videos unlock some tools. You choose whether to watch them.

Two features go through an outside service, only when you start them: the Speed test (Cloudflare) and optional videos (Google AdMob). Privacy policy

With your PC

Your phone and PC talk to each other

Pair Vectorem for Windows by scanning a QR code: notes, tasks and streaks in sync, photos sent to the PC, a remote. Over your Wi-Fi, encrypted, no account.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Install, pick your tools, that's it.

Which Android version?

Android 8.0 or later.

What if I change phones?

Settings › Data & security: export a backup to a file, then import it on the new phone.

Are there ads?

No ad is forced on you. Optional videos unlock some tools, only if you choose to watch them.

Why “beta”?

Vectorem changes fast and testers use it every day. Some bugs may remain: write to support@vectorem.app, every message is read.

Do I need the PC version?

No, the Android app works on its own. The PC adds sync, file sending and a remote.
